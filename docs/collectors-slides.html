Option 1

Burner account pool

We own ~10 throwaway accounts. One gets blocked, the next takes over.

Cost
$10–40 / month
accounts + proxies
Build
1–2 weeks
on top of what exists
Risk
Breaks Instagram’s terms
Meta has sued scrapers

Cost. Accounts are free to create, or $0.40–3 each to buy pre-aged. Aged accounts survive longer; fresh ones get challenged within hours. Expect ~25% to die each month. Proxies: about 1 GB/month at $4–8/GB, one residential IP per account.

Option 2

Vendor API

A scraping company fetches the posts. We pay per post.

Cost
$75–115 / month
Bright Data or Apify; HikerAPI under $50
Build
2–4 days
the slot already exists
Risk
Vendor carries it
no accounts on our side

Cost. Bright Data $1.50 and Apify $2.30 per 1,000 posts, pay as you go. HikerAPI ~$0.001 per request after a $100 deposit. Prices checked 27 Aug 2026. Coverage depends on the vendor; “newest first” hashtag results need checking before we commit.

Option 3

Claude drives a browser

An AI agent opens Instagram and reads each post, like a person would.

Cost
~$3,000 / month
about 6¢ a post
Build
1–2 weeks
plus constant tuning
Risk
Same as option 1
still needs a logged-in account

Cost. Instagram’s hashtag grid shows only thumbnails, so the agent must open every post: about 2 model calls of ~10k tokens each on Sonnet 5 ≈ 6¢. Haiku 4.5 halves it; screenshot-based control costs 3–5× more. It still logs in with one of our accounts, so the ban risk is the same as option 1.

Monthly bill at 50,000 posts

Detection costs the same. Collection doesn’t.

Detection · $520, every option Collection

Volume. 29 terms × 60 posts × one sweep a day ≈ 50,000 posts/month; double every bar for two sweeps. Detection is $0.0104 per post, measured from our own runs on Sonnet 5, and is the biggest line for options 1 and 2.
